HOW TO PLAY

  • Drag back from your bow to pull the arrow. A dotted line shows where it will fly (Levels 1–3 only — after that, aim by feel).
  • Release to shoot. Arrows fly with real gravity, so aim a little high.
  • Headshot = instant kill (+100). Body hits hurt: 2 hits to kill (+50).
  • Enemies hide on trees and rooftops and shoot back. Each hit costs you 25 HP.
  • Kill every enemy to clear the level. 70 levels, faster and deadlier as you climb.
  • Unlock weapons as you level up — or buy them early with coins. 🔥 Flame arrows burn, ❄️ Ice freezes, 💣 Bombs clear groups. 🧲 Seeking curves to the nearest enemy, 🔱 Shadow Piercer passes through a line of them — those two are earned by reaching Level 45 / 70, never for sale.
  • Pick your weapon on the WEAPONS page from the main menu — it stays equipped until you are killed. Change it from the Game Over screen.
  • Your progress, coins and weapons save automatically on this device.
  • Wind bends your arrows — watch the WIND meter. Bosses appear every 10 levels. Spend coins in the Shop on health, speed and blast upgrades, and chase achievements.
  • From Level 11, a heart ❤️ or shield 🛡️ sometimes falls from the sky — shoot it to collect it. Hearts heal +25 HP, shields block one hit.
